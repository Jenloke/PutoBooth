How the photo booth works

Photobooth turns your phone or computer into a photo booth: a countdown, a few photos in a row, and a finished strip at the end. It runs in your browser, with nothing to install and no account.

1. Start the camera

Tap Start camera and allow access when your browser asks. It works in Safari and Chrome on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows. On a Mac you can also pick an external webcam or your iPhone (Continuity Camera) in the settings.

Photos are taken and processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded. No camera, or it's blocked? Choose Use photos instead to make the strip from photos you already have.

2. Pick a layout

The classic strip is the default. Print layouts are made at 300 DPI, so they print sharp at their real size.

3. Shoot

Tap the shutter, or press Space on a keyboard. A countdown of 3, 5 or 10 seconds runs before each photo, with a pause you choose between shots (half a second to five seconds). Press Esc to stop.

  • Screen flash turns the screen white for each photo, which lights faces in a dim room.
  • Mirror photos saves selfies the way the preview shows them.
  • Shutter sound adds countdown beeps and a click.
  • Grid overlay shows rule-of-thirds guides while you line up.

4. Make it yours

  • Style: a filter (B&W, Noir, Sepia, Warm, Cool, Vintage or Vivid), a frame color, spacing and rounded corners.
  • Text: a caption in one of five fonts, and the date.
  • Logo: your own image in the footer, handy for weddings and events.
  • Retakes: tap any photo to retake just that one.

The preview is the file you'll save, pixel for pixel, so what you see is what you get.

5. Save, share or print

Download it as a PNG or JPEG. On iPhone, tap Share, then Save Image to put it in Photos. Print it at its actual size, or turn on Print sheet to get two strips side by side on 4×6 paper, ready to cut.

Results also stay in the Gallery on this device, where you can reopen and restyle them. Save the ones you want to keep: browsers can clear website storage.

Shoot with someone else

Two devices can share one booth, in the same room or far apart: each of you fills half of every photo, and the countdown runs on both screens at once. See how shooting together works.

Keep it on your home screen

On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, choose Install. It opens full screen and works offline after the first visit.

Ready? The booth opens right in your browser.
