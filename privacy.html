Privacy

In short: your photos never leave your device, except to go straight to the other person's device in a shared booth. There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies.

Photos

The camera, the countdown, the filters and the finished image all run in your browser. Photos are never uploaded to a server, and that includes photos you pick from your library.

What stays on your device

  • Gallery: your recent results and their photos, in your browser's storage (IndexedDB), so you can reopen them. Delete them in the Gallery, or by clearing this site's data.
  • Settings: layout, countdown, style, caption and display name, in your browser's local storage.
  • Logo: if you add one, it's kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) too.

Browsers can clear website storage, Safari especially for sites you haven't used in a while, so save the strips you want to keep.

Shared booth

In a shared booth, photos and live video go directly between the two devices, encrypted by WebRTC. Your display name goes only to the other device.

  • To find each other, the devices exchange a few small messages through the PeerJS cloud server (0.peerjs.com). It sees the room code and the devices' network addresses, never photos or video.
  • Google's STUN server tells each device its public network address.
  • When a network blocks a direct connection, PeerJS's relay (TURN) servers forward the traffic, which stays encrypted and unreadable to them.

What we count

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to see how many people visit and how fast pages load. Neither uses cookies. They record the page, the site you came from, your country, browser and device type, page-load timings, and a few anonymous steps, such as "camera started" or "strip saved". They never receive photos, names, captions or room codes. Vercel tells visits apart with a hash of the request that it discards after 24 hours.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, handles your requests (including your IP address) to deliver the pages. Fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.
