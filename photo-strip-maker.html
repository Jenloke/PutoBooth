Make a 2×6 photo booth strip

The classic photo booth strip is 2 by 6 inches, with four photos stacked one above the other. Photobooth makes them in your browser, from your camera or from photos you already have, ready to print.

Make a strip

Make one in a minute

  1. Open the booth. Classic Strip is already selected.
  2. Tap Start camera, then the shutter: a countdown and four photos. Or choose Use photos instead and pick four photos from your library.
  3. Pick a filter, a frame color and a caption.
  4. Download, share or print it.

Print-ready sizes

Every strip is made at 300 DPI: 600 × 1800 pixels for a 2×6 strip. Most home photo printers and print shops use 4×6 paper, so turn on Print sheet (in the Export tab) to put two strips side by side on one 4×6 page. Print it, then cut down the middle.

Style it

  • Filters: B&W, Noir, Sepia, Warm, Cool, Vintage and Vivid.
  • Frame: eight preset colors or any color you like, with adjustable spacing and rounded corners.
  • Caption: up to 40 characters, in a serif, sans, handwritten, display or script font.
  • Date and logo: stamp the date, and add your own logo to the footer.

Ideas

  • Weddings and parties: set the caption and logo once. They stay for every strip, so guests just tap the shutter.
  • Far apart: make one strip with someone on another device. Shoot together.
  • Cards and keepsakes: print strips at home on 4×6 photo paper, two at a time.
